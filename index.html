Lumetra is a RAW editor
built for speed.

A custom engine written in Rust, with the precision of Canon's own software. Free and open source.

Coming soon

One window for the whole job.

Lumetra opens a card or a folder of Canon RAW files, lets you sort and rate them, develop them with the controls Canon shooters already know, and send them out as JPEG, TIFF, HEIF or prints. The grid and the editor are two modes of the same window, so moving between them never reloads anything.

Underneath is Lumetra's own engine, written in Rust: CR3 decoding, demosaicing, colour and tone, lens correction, noise reduction. It reproduces Canon's processing exactly. The same settings give the same file as Digital Photo Professional 4, byte for byte, and a change shows in about a tenth of a second.

Workflow.

01

Cull

Thumbnails come straight from the previews inside each RAW file, so a folder of twelve fresh CR3s fills in 0.13 s. Rate, reject and mark from the keyboard.

  • RAW and same-name JPEG/HEIF shown as one picture
  • Collections across folders
  • Rename by template, with sidecars and a one-step undo
  • Quick check and side-by-side compare of up to four pictures
02

Develop

The palette follows Canon's: brightness, white balance, Picture Styles, contrast, highlight and shadow, a per-channel tone curve, Auto Lighting Optimizer and the eight-axis colour adjustment.

  • Lens distortion, vignetting and chromatic aberration from lensfun
  • Crop with aspect presets, straighten, vertical and horizontal perspective
  • Dust delete and copy stamp
  • Neural-network noise reduction on the raw mosaic, 0.45 s on a GPU
  • JPEG and TIFF files edited with the same tone and colour tools
03

Beyond one frame

Brackets, bursts and Dual Pixel files are first-class, not exports to another program.

  • HDR from a bracket, aligned and deghosted, with tone-mapping presets
  • Multiple exposure: add, average, weighted, lighten, darken
  • Focus stacking from a focus bracket
  • RAW burst rolls: save chosen frames as standalone CR3s, bit for bit
  • Dual Pixel RAW: micro-adjustment, bokeh shift, ghosting reduction
04

Deliver

Exports run in a queue in the background while you keep working.

  • JPEG, TIFF 8/16-bit, PNG, HEIF 8 and 10-bit, HDR HEIF in BT.2020 PQ
  • CMYK TIFF and JPEG through any ICC profile, soft proofing with gamut warning
  • Prints and contact sheets with captions
  • IPTC title, keywords, creator and location written into every export

You have a GPU.
Why not use it.

Lumetra splits the work by what it needs. Anything that has to match to the byte runs in exact integer maths. Everything that doesn't runs on your graphics card, through compute shaders on DirectX 12 or Vulkan, with the CPU as a fallback.

25× neural-network noise reduction on a 24 MP raw file
0.45 s on the GPU against 11.5 s on the CPU
  • Live previewColour, tone, demosaic and lens correction drawn on the GPU while you edit.
  • Noise reductionThe neural network runs on the raw mosaic as two GPU kernels, matching the CPU result to within a millionth.
  • Exact where it countsThe file you export is computed precisely, so the GPU never costs you a byte.

Reads what the camera wrote.

White-balance presets
Daylight, shade, cloudy, tungsten, fluorescent and flash use your camera's own levels from the maker notes, not a generic temperature.
AF points
Shown over the picture, red where the camera focused, on RAW files and on the camera's JPEG and HEIF.
Picture Style
The style you shot with is the starting point, including User Defined 1–3 from your camera.
Aspect crop
Shot in 16:9 or 1:1? The picture opens framed that way, with the full sensor still there.
Dual Pixel RAW
The second image inside a Dual Pixel file is read for micro-adjustment, bokeh shift and ghosting reduction.
Burst rolls and RAW movies
Every frame of a RAW burst or CRM movie can be opened, developed or saved on its own.
Lens
The lens name in the file picks its correction profile automatically.
Ratings
Ratings and rejects can go into each file's XMP, where other software reads them.

Or no window at all

Everything the app does is also a command, for scripts and batch jobs.

# a shoot to full-size JPEGs, Landscape style
lumetra-cli develop D:\Shoot\*.CR3 -o out --style landscape

# 16-bit TIFF in Adobe RGB, keeping the recipe
lumetra-cli develop IMG_0001.CR3 -o out -f tiff16 --space adobe-rgb --save-recipe

# HDR from a bracket, focus stack from another
lumetra-cli hdr IMG_1.CR3 IMG_2.CR3 IMG_3.CR3 -o hdr.tif
lumetra-cli stack IMG_4.CR3 IMG_5.CR3 IMG_6.CR3 -o stack.tif

# rename a trip by date, preview first
lumetra-cli rename D:\Trip -t "{date}_{seq:4}" --sort date

Questions.

Does it change my RAW files?

No. Edits live in a small sidecar file next to each picture. Lumetra writes into a RAW only when you ask it to: saving settings into the file the way Canon's software does, or with ratings-in-file switched on.

Which cameras?

Canon CR3, CR2 and CRW files. It has been developed and checked on EOS R50 and R50 V files, with EOS R5 and R7 samples for Dual Pixel RAW.

Do I need Digital Photo Professional?

No. Lumetra is its own program with its own engine. It matches DPP 4's output to the byte without needing DPP installed.

Mac or Linux?

Windows 10 and 11 for now.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Lumetra is open source under the LGPL-2.1-or-later.

When can I get it?

It isn't released yet. The installer and the source will be linked here.
